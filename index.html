Knee / Quad Release

3 exercises
Quads Knee rehab Chair Resistance band

01 Sit-Down Squat with Band

Reps8–12
Sets3
Progression+2 reps weekly
  1. Place your feet shoulder-width apart, or just outside it.
  2. Tie a resistance band around your knees, kept quite tight.
  3. Stand in front of a chair and slowly bend your knees.
  4. Keep a good curve in your lower back and control the descent onto the chair.
  5. To stand, push up through your legs and straighten your knees.
Cue

The band's job is to stop your knees caving inward — push out against it the whole time. If there's no reaction after a week, add 2 reps.

02 Split Squat

Reps5 each leg
Progression+1 rep weekly
  1. Take a step forwards, as if going into a lunge.
  2. Drop the back knee towards the ground.
  3. The front knee stays at 90 degrees and does not travel forward past that point.
  4. Keep your knee stacked over the second ray of your foot — never let it drop inwards.
  5. Push back up to the start position.
Cue

Only add a rep each week if there's no adverse reaction from the knee. Alignment matters more than depth here.

03 Quadriceps Stretch

Hold30 seconds
Reps5
  1. Hold something sturdy — a wall, table or chair — if you're unsteady.
  2. Tighten your stomach and buttock muscles.
  3. Pull your foot up towards your bottom.
  4. Hold the stretch, then swap sides.
Cue

You should feel this on the front of the thigh — not as pain in the knee joint. You can also do this lying on your side.

01 Clam Advanced with Band

Reps15
Sets3
  1. Lie on your side with both knees bent.
  2. Place a band around your thighs, just above the knees.
  3. Draw your belly button inwards to engage your deep abdominal muscles.
  4. Keeping your feet together, lift them a few inches off the floor.
  5. Open your knees like a clam, hold, then return to the start.
Cue

If it's too tough, drop the band entirely or lengthen it. Keep your hips stacked — don't let your top hip roll backwards to cheat the movement.

02 Bird Dog Quadruped with Hip Abduction Band

Reps15
Sets3
  1. Go onto all fours with a band around your thighs.
  2. Slightly lift your knees off the floor and hold them hovering.
  3. Move one hip outwards against the band.
  4. Return to the start position under control.
Cue

This diagram is drawn looking down from above so you can see the hip travel. Keep your back flat and hips level — the movement comes from the hip, not from twisting your torso.

03 Bird Dog One Leg

Reps10–15
ThenPulse × 15
SidesEach side
  1. Position yourself on all fours, on the floor or your bed, with good posture.
  2. Bring the knee forward, then lead with the knee out to the side (the "dog pee" position).
  3. From there, extend into a straight leg behind you. Repeat for 10–15 reps.
  4. Then hold the straight leg out, turn the foot outward, and pulse 15 times.
  5. Repeat everything on the other side.
Cue

Don't let your lower back arch as the leg goes back — the leg only needs to reach level with your torso, no higher.

04 Bulgarian Split Squat

Reps10–12 each side
Sets2–3
LoadBodyweight
  1. Place one foot on a chair or bench behind you, the other flat on the floor.
  2. Lean your body slightly forward to bias the glutes.
  3. Go into a squat by bending your front knee as far as is comfortable.
  4. Keep that knee in line with the middle of your foot — no drifting inwards or outwards.
  5. Straighten your leg to return to the start position.
Cue

The forward lean is what shifts the work from quads to glutes. Staying bolt upright turns this back into a quad exercise.

05 Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

  1. Grip the bar and stand tall, bar against the front of your thighs.
  2. Push your hips back, keeping the bar close to your body throughout the descent.
  3. Keep a straight back while hinging at the hips.
  4. Lower the bar to shin level.
  5. Stand up tall by pushing your hips forward and engaging the glutes.
Cue

This is a hip hinge, not a squat — your knees stay almost still. If the bar drifts away from your legs, you've turned it into a back exercise.

06 Single Leg Kettlebell Deadlift

  1. Set up with your feet shoulder-width apart, one hand on the kettlebell in an overhand grip.
  2. Balance on one foot.
  3. Hinge at the hip, keeping the kettlebell close to your body throughout the descent.
  4. Keep a straight back and lower the kettlebell to shin level.
  5. Stand up by pushing your hips forward and engaging the glutes.
Cue

Your body and the free leg should form one straight line from head to heel. If you wobble, slow down rather than shortening the range.

Hamstrings Knee rehab Floor / mat

07 Eccentric Hamstring Curl, Double-Leg Supine

  1. Lie down on your back and bend both knees.
  2. Push your heels down into the floor.
  3. Drag your heels along the floor towards your buttocks, keeping that downward pressure.
  4. Return to the start under control.
Cue

The downward heel pressure is what creates the contraction in the back of your thighs. Without it, your legs just slide and the hamstrings do nothing.

Glutes Strengthening

2 exercises
Glutes Quads Adductors Hack squat machine

01 Wide Stance Hack Squat

  1. Set your shoulders under the pads with your back flat against the support.
  2. Place your feet wide on the platform, toes turned slightly outward.
  3. Unrack the weight and lower under control, letting your knees track over your toes.
  4. Descend until your thighs are at least parallel to the platform.
  5. Drive through your heels and outer feet to press back up to the start.
Cue

The wider your stance, the more load shifts onto your glutes and inner thighs. Don't let your knees cave inward as you press up.
